Two of the live FAQs are kept ("What is Create with AI?" and "How do I get started?") and the rest are replaced with six keyword-targeted, PAA-aligned questions, for eight total. Backed by FAQPage schema.

What is Create with AI?
Create with AI is Beautiful.ai's guided AI presentation workflow. It helps you move from an initial prompt or source material to a polished presentation in stages, so you can shape the story, refine the structure, and iterate on slides with more control.
How do I get started?
Click Create Presentation in Beautiful.ai, then choose Create with AI. You can start with whatever you already have, including a short prompt, a detailed brief, a pasted outline, or notes and source material for added context.
What is the best AI presentation maker?
The best AI presentation maker depends on your workflow. Beautiful.ai stands out for teams who want a guided creation process rather than a one-shot generator. With Create with AI, you shape the outline before the design begins, which means fewer surprises and less rework.
Can you use AI to make a presentation?
Yes. AI presentation makers like Beautiful.ai let you generate a full deck from a text prompt, document, or outline. Beautiful.ai's AI presentation generator adds a review step between the outline and the final design, so the result reflects your actual content and goals.
Can ChatGPT create presentations?
ChatGPT can write outlines and slide copy, but it cannot produce a designed deck on its own. Beautiful.ai handles the full workflow: paste an outline from ChatGPT, and Create with AI structures, designs, and formats it inside one platform.
Is Beautiful.ai free to use?
Beautiful.ai offers a free trial so you can explore the AI presentation maker before committing to a plan. Paid plans are available for individuals, teams, and enterprise organizations.
How do I choose an AI presentation maker?
When choosing an AI presentation maker, consider workflow fit, design quality, brand control, and export options (does it work as an AI PPT maker that exports to PowerPoint?). Beautiful.ai is built for organizations where quality and brand consistency matter, not just speed.
Can Beautiful.ai export to PowerPoint?
Yes. Every deck can be exported as a PowerPoint file, so you can use it as your AI PPT maker or AI PowerPoint generator and still deliver in the format your audience expects. Slides are fully editable after export.
